BellSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Privacy at BellSync

BellSync does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Schedule information, imported photos or PDFs, reminder settings, room numbers, lunch assignments, and other BellSync configuration information are processed and stored locally on the user's device.

Photos and PDF Imports

BellSync allows users to import schedule information from photos and PDF documents. These files are used to help create the user's BellSync schedule and are not uploaded to BellSync servers.

Notifications

BellSync uses Apple's notification system to provide optional bell reminders. Notification permissions are controlled by the user through iOS settings.

Apple Watch

BellSync may share schedule and configuration information between the user's iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's system frameworks. This information is used only to provide BellSync functionality.

Analytics and Advertising

BellSync does not use advertising networks, tracking technologies, analytics SDKs, or third-party services that collect user data.

Support Communications

If you contact BellSync support by email, the information you provide is used only to respond to your support request.
