BellSync Support

Help and support for BellSync.

Need help?

BellSync helps teachers and school staff manage rotating schedules, bell countdowns, reminders, and Apple Watch complications.

For support, questions, or to report a problem, email:
support@bellsync.app

Common Topics

  • Importing or replacing a schedule
  • Bell reminders and notification permissions
  • Apple Watch setup
  • Schedule or lunch-assignment changes
  • Reporting incorrect bell times

When contacting support

Please include your device type, iOS or watchOS version, and a short description of the problem.
